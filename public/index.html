Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about billing, scheduling, and tutor matchmaking.

Can I switch between plans at any time?

Yes. You can upgrade, downgrade, or cancel your monthly membership at any time with no lock-in fees or penalties.

What if a session doesn't meet my expectations?

We offer a 100% Satisfaction Guarantee. If you are not satisfied with your first session, we will credit your account in full or reassign you to another educator.

Do unused monthly hours roll over?

Yes. In the Scholar and Honors plans, unused tutoring hours automatically roll over for up to 60 days as long as your membership is active.

How are tutors paid?

Educators receive direct electronic payouts within 2 business days of session completion and verification, ensuring top-tier reliability.

Mandatory Academic Guidelines ⚖️

Aviora Education Subscription Terms & Conditions

Please read these subscription provisions carefully before purchasing or booking sessions. By enrolling in an Aviora subscription package, you agree to comply with the terms set forth below.

1. Strict No-Refund Policy

All subscription packages (4, 8, or 12 lessons) and paid trial bookings are strictly non-refundable once purchased or activated. Fees cannot be prorated, refunded, or credited back for unused portions.

2. Upfront Payment Requirement

Full package fees must be paid upfront prior to booking sessions or reserving educator calendar slots. Lesson balances are credited immediately upon confirmed payment.

3. One-Month Expiration & Validity

Every subscription package is valid for exactly one calendar month (30 days) from the date and timestamp of purchase. Any unused lessons remaining after the expiration date will lapse and cannot be rolled over.

4. Student-Determined Scheduling

Students possess complete autonomy to set and schedule lesson dates and times based on faculty availability. Rescheduling requires a minimum of 3 hours notice before the class to preserve session credits.

5. Paid Trial Lesson — Strictly 1-Time Use

Trial lessons are paid at the rate of $39 and are strictly limited to one single trial per registered student. Repeated purchases of trial lessons are restricted by platform validation.

6. Multi-Subject & Tutor Flexibility

Your subscription lessons may be distributed across any approved Aviora faculty members and academic disciplines (Mathematics, Sciences, Humanities, Languages, and Coding).

7. Class Duration Limit

Note: All subjects are a maximum of 50 minutes of class per session to ensure focused, high-retention learning.
