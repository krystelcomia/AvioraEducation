Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about billing, scheduling, and tutor matchmaking.

Can I switch between plans at any time?

Yes. You can upgrade, downgrade, or cancel your monthly membership at any time with no lock-in fees or penalties.

What if a session doesn't meet my expectations?

We offer a 100% Satisfaction Guarantee. If you are not satisfied with your first session, we will credit your account in full or reassign you to another educator.

Do unused monthly hours roll over?

Yes. In the Scholar and Honors plans, unused tutoring hours automatically roll over for up to 60 days as long as your membership is active.

How are tutors paid?

Educators receive direct electronic payouts within 2 business days of session completion and verification, ensuring top-tier reliability.
