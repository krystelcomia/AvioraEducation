Mandatory Academic Guidelines

Aviora Education Subscription Terms & Conditions

Please read these subscription provisions carefully before purchasing or booking sessions. By enrolling in an Aviora subscription package, you agree to comply with the terms set forth below.

1. Strict No-Refund Policy

All subscription packages (4, 8, or 12 lessons) and trial enrollments are strictly non-refundable once purchased or activated. Fees cannot be prorated, refunded, or credited back for unused portions.

2. Upfront Payment Requirement

Full package fees must be paid upfront prior to booking sessions or reserving educator calendar slots. Lesson balances are credited immediately upon confirmed payment.

3. One-Month Expiration & Validity

Every subscription package is valid for exactly one calendar month (30 days) from the date and timestamp of purchase. Any unused lessons remaining after the expiration date will lapse and cannot be rolled over.

4. Student-Determined Scheduling

Students possess complete autonomy to set and schedule lesson dates and times based on faculty availability. Rescheduling requires a minimum of 3 hours notice before the class to preserve session credits.

5. Free Trial Lesson ($0) — Strictly 1-Time Use

The trial is a one-time offer for $0 covering a single 30-minute lesson and is strictly limited to one single trial per registered student. Once a student has used it, this option is no longer available. Repeated claims of trial lessons are restricted by platform validation.

6. Multi-Subject & Tutor Flexibility

Your subscription lessons may be distributed across any approved Aviora faculty members and academic disciplines (Mathematics, Sciences, Humanities, Languages, and Coding).

7. Class Duration Limit

Note: Trial lessons are 30 minutes in length. All standard subscription subjects are a maximum of 50 minutes of class per session to ensure focused, high-retention learning.

Billing & Subscription FAQ

Everything you need to know about getting started, scheduling, and package validity.

How do I get started if I don't have an account yet?

Simply click on any package above—such as the Free Trial or 4 Lessons Package. You will be prompted to create your student account, and your selected package will be automatically opened for you to choose your educator and schedule your lessons.

How is subscription package pricing calculated?

Your package price is calculated transparently upon setup based on the number of lessons (4, 8, or 12) multiplied by your selected verified educator's hourly rate, paid upfront before classes begin.

What is the validity period of the lesson packages?

All subscription packages are valid for exactly 1 calendar month (30 days) from the purchase date. Lessons can be scheduled flexibly anytime within this 30-day window.

Can I reschedule a session if something comes up?

Yes. Students have complete autonomy to reschedule lessons as long as notice is submitted at least 3 hours before the scheduled class start time.
