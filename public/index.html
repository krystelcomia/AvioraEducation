Institutional Heritage & Governance

About Aviora Education

Founded on the principle that transformative academic achievement begins with world-class, personalized mentorship tailored to each student's unique potential.

Purpose & Commitment

Our Mission

Our mission is to provide accessible, high-quality, and personalized education to learners around the world through engaging and flexible online learning experiences. We strive to understand the unique needs, goals, and learning styles of every student by connecting them with dedicated educators and meaningful learning opportunities.

Beginning with English language education and expanding into a diverse range of academic subjects, we aim to empower learners to develop essential knowledge, strengthen their skills, build confidence, and reach their full potential. Through innovative teaching approaches and a commitment to continuous improvement, we seek to make learning more inclusive, meaningful, and accessible, regardless of geographical boundaries.

Global Aspiration

Our Vision

Our vision is to become a globally recognized and trusted online learning platform originating from the Philippines, connecting learners and educators across countries and cultures through the power of education.

We aspire to grow beyond language instruction into a comprehensive learning platform that offers diverse educational opportunities to students of different ages, backgrounds, and aspirations. By embracing innovation, inclusivity, and educational excellence, we envision a world where geographical location does not limit access to knowledge, personal growth, or academic success. Through our commitment to lifelong learning and global connection, we aim to make a meaningful and lasting contribution to the future of education.

Our Brand Identity ✦ Global Outlook

Learning Beyond Borders

Rooted in the Philippines. Connecting learners to a world of possibilities.

Our identity reflects our belief that education should not be limited by geography, background, or circumstance. As a platform built in the Philippines with a global outlook, we aim to bring quality learning experiences to international communities while continuously broadening the subjects, skills, and opportunities we offer.

Founder

As a Psychology student, she recognizes the importance of understanding how people think, learn, and develop. Common Inquiries

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about scheduling, interactive classes, Aviora Buddy, and educator onboarding.

How does 1-on-1 session booking work?

Browse vetted educators by subject specialization, hourly rate, and weekly availability timetable. You can book individual sessions with complete schedule sovereignty, enroll in 4, 8, or 12-lesson monthly packages, or start with a 30-minute free trial ($0).

How does Aviora Buddy co-learning work?

Aviora Buddy connects two students in an interactive classroom for a flat $6 fee without tutors. Submitted bookings remain in pending status while our administrator coordinates student pairings and sets the confirmed schedule, notifying both students immediately.

How are educators accredited and verified?

Every educator submits their academic resume and university diploma. Our administration conducts a credential audit and virtual interview before granting full operational approval, ensuring empathetic and qualified instruction.

Can students schedule a free trial lesson?

Yes! First-time students can book a complimentary 30-minute diagnostic session ($0 flat, strictly 1-time use) with an educator to evaluate fit and establish their learning roadmap before committing to a package.

What subjects are available on Aviora Education?

Beginning with comprehensive English language education, Aviora offers instruction across calculus, physics, chemistry, computer science & coding, humanities, test preparation, and collegiate essay writing.

What happens during an interactive Aviora session?

Sessions are conducted in our high-definition interactive classroom with real-time video, collaborative whiteboard tools, private messaging, and comprehensive session feedback saved in your student portal.

Mandatory Academic Guidelines

Aviora Education Subscription Terms & Conditions

Please read these subscription provisions carefully before purchasing or booking sessions. By enrolling in an Aviora subscription package, you agree to comply with the terms set forth below.

1. Strict No-Refund Policy

All subscription packages (4, 8, or 12 lessons) and trial enrollments are strictly non-refundable once purchased or activated. Fees cannot be prorated, refunded, or credited back for unused portions.

2. Upfront Payment Requirement

Full package fees must be paid upfront prior to booking sessions or reserving educator calendar slots. Lesson balances are credited immediately upon confirmed payment.

3. One-Month Expiration & Validity

Every subscription package is valid for exactly one calendar month (30 days) from the date and timestamp of purchase. Any unused lessons remaining after the expiration date will lapse and cannot be rolled over.

4. Student-Determined Scheduling

Students possess complete autonomy to set and schedule lesson dates and times based on faculty availability. Rescheduling requires a minimum of 3 hours notice before the class to preserve session credits.

5. Free Trial Lesson ($0) — Strictly 1-Time Use

The trial is a one-time offer for $0 covering a single 30-minute lesson and is strictly limited to one single trial per registered student. Once a student has used it, this option is no longer available. Repeated claims of trial lessons are restricted by platform validation.

6. Multi-Subject & Tutor Flexibility

Your subscription lessons may be distributed across any approved Aviora faculty members and academic disciplines (Mathematics, Sciences, Humanities, Languages, and Coding).

7. Class Duration Limit

Note: Trial lessons are 30 minutes in length. All standard subscription subjects are a maximum of 50 minutes of class per session to ensure focused, high-retention learning.

Billing & Subscription FAQ

Everything you need to know about getting started, scheduling, and package validity.

How do I get started if I don't have an account yet?

Simply click on any package above—such as the Free Trial or 4 Lessons Package. You will be prompted to create your student account, and your selected package will be automatically opened for you to choose your educator and schedule your lessons.

How is subscription package pricing calculated?

Your package price is calculated transparently upon setup based on the number of lessons (4, 8, or 12) multiplied by your selected verified educator's hourly rate, paid upfront before classes begin.

What is the validity period of the lesson packages?

All subscription packages are valid for exactly 1 calendar month (30 days) from the purchase date. Lessons can be scheduled flexibly anytime within this 30-day window.

Can I reschedule a session if something comes up?

Yes. Students have complete autonomy to reschedule lessons as long as notice is submitted at least 3 hours before the scheduled class start time.
